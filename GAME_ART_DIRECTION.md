# Game Art Direction — locked reference

The game should look like a **cozy low-poly 3D forest MOBA** (reference:
"Fable of Legends" screenshots supplied by Hector, 2026-07-17), NOT neon
sci-fi. Every future visual pass on the game (web or Roblox) follows this.

## World

- Soft daylight meadow: mid-green grass with subtle darker patches.
- Scattered low-poly pine trees (flat-shaded, stacked triangle canopies),
  occasional rocks and a small pond.
- Wide tan **dirt paths** cutting diagonally through the map; bases sit in
  dirt clearings ringed by wooden fence posts.
- Soft blob shadows under every character/prop. No neon glow, no grid lines.

## Characters

- Chibi proportions: big head, small body, tiny feet. Team-colored hoods and
  clothes, light skin, simple dot eyes.
- Team palette: player blue `#4a78d0`; rivals red `#d05348`,
  purple `#9a6ad0`, orange `#e0883a`.

## UI

- **Wooden panels**: brown wood gradient (`#8a5f3a` → `#6b4527`), dark
  `#4a2f1a` borders, generous rounded corners, soft drop shadow.
- **Parchment** inner surfaces `#f3e3c2` with dark-brown ink text `#3a2a18`.
- **Gold** accents and buttons (`#f4c35a` → `#d99a2e`), used for prices,
  coins, and big announcement text ("KILLING SPREE"-style: chunky gold
  letters with dark outline).
- Rounded friendly type (ui-rounded/system), heavy weights; monospace
  tabular numerals for counters.
